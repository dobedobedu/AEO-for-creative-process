# Stage-Specific Key Questions

## Explore Stage
KEY QUESTIONS FOR EXPLORE STAGE: Are we being discovered? Where do we appear in lists? What's the first impression?

## Consider Stage
KEY QUESTIONS FOR CONSIDER STAGE: What sentiment do AI models express? Are we portrayed positively? What concerns are raised?

## Compare Stage
KEY QUESTIONS FOR COMPARE STAGE: How do we stack up against competitors? Are comparisons favorable? Who wins head-to-head?

## Decide Stage
KEY QUESTIONS FOR DECIDE STAGE: Are we being recommended? How strong are the recommendations? What's the final impression?
